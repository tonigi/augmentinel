How to play

Absorb trees and gain height to reach the Sentinel. Create a robot on higher ground, then transfer into it. Keep out of the Sentinel’s gaze.

Enter advances the title and selects a landscape. ← → select landscapes or turn; ↑ ↓ look up/down. Click the game for mouse look.
A / left click absorb · T tree · B / right click boulder · R / middle click robot · Q transfer · H hyperspace · U turn around · P pause.
M music · N tunes · + / − music volume. Progress and preferences save in this browser.

Augmentinel by Simon Owen · Browser port uses Emscripten, WebGL 2 and Web Audio · Source / GPLv3. Desktop keyboard and mouse required. VR is available in the Windows version.
